Star Dash: Nine Worlds — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026 · Version 1.1

1. General

Star Dash: Nine Worlds is a single-player game. We follow the principle of minimum necessity: we do not collect, store, or share any personal information. The only online behaviour is the update check (see Section 2); apart from that, the game is fully offline.

2. Information we collect

None. This game:

  • requires no registration or login, and collects no identity information such as names, phone numbers, or email addresses;
  • does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photo permissions;
  • integrates no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs or other third-party data services;
  • keeps game progress and settings only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them entirely; nothing is uploaded to any server.
  • When checking for updates, the app only fetches a version information file (version number and download address) from the public update repository to compare whether a newer version exists; only after you confirm an update does it download the new installer package. No personal information is collected or uploaded at any point in this process.

3. Use and sharing of information

Because we collect no information, there is no use, sale, or sharing of your personal information with any third party.

4. Children's privacy

This game is suitable for all ages. It contains no social features, no payment prompts, and no external links, and it does not knowingly collect information from children.

5. Policy updates

The only online feature added in this version is the update check (see Section 2). If a future version adds any other online feature, we will update this policy first, highlight the change in the app, and only enable the feature after obtaining your consent.

6. Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us through the developer contact details shown on the app's store page.

7. Originality statement

The game's name, starship design, artwork, text, and audio are all original. The game uses no trademarks or assets of any real space agency or company; planet names and astronomical facts are public scientific knowledge.
